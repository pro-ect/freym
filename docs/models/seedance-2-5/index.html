Seedance 2.5

Seedance 2.5 is ByteDance's AI video generation model, notable for native audio and voice generation in a single pass. Within days of release it became the fastest-adopted video model of mid-2026: Hedra, Leonardo, Higgsfield, Pika, Runway and fal.ai all announced integrations between late July and early August 2026.
