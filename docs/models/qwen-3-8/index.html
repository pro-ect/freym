Qwen 3.8 & Qwen 3.8-Max

Qwen 3.8 is Alibaba's flagship large language model, launched July 19, 2026 with 2.4 trillion parameters and a commitment to open weights. Qwen 3.8-Max, released August 3, 2026, is described by Alibaba as its most capable model yet — ranking #5 on the AI Index, #1 on the Agentic Index, and #2 in the Image-to-WebDev Arena, at a lower cost than its predecessors.
